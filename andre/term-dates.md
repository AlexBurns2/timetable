# Term dates for NSW public schools

Reference for the week numbers on Andre's page (`TERMS` in `andre/index.html`).
The school is in Sydney, so it follows the **Eastern division** dates. The code
stores each term as its first and last day for students. Days outside them
(holidays, and pupil-free days before a term starts) show no lessons. A term
starting mid-week (e.g. Wednesday 22 April) counts that whole week as Week 1.
When a new year's dates come out, add them here and to `TERMS`.
