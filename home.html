    About

    What this is

    MSC Unofficial is a free upgrade to the school timetable, inspired by the work of OggyP. The website is strictly for utility and entertainment: there will never be any ads, payments or data collection.

    How it was made

    Put bluntly, the website was almost entirely coded by large language models with minimal human supervision. We guided the general functionality, look and details of the website; Claude Opus 5.5 wrote and implemented our ideas. Of course, we would much rather be able to proclaim the website as the hard work of humans alone, but with other commitments and the purpose of the website in mind, we felt that the extensive use of AI was justified. For this reason, we haven't included our names anywhere on the website or credits.

    What this means for you

    AI generated code is prone to mistakes and blindspots. If you catch any such issues, please report them through the "Suggest a feature" button on the homepage. Privacy and data management was supervised by us. All important data is encrypted and stored with row level security on a remote server. We can't access this private information either, only you can access it with your specific credentials.

    AI and your data

    No AIs are involved in the actual function and access of the site, AI was ONLY used to code it. Your data is never sent to any kind of AI when you access the site. AI was strictly used for development alone.
